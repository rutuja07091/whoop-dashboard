Churn-risk ranking by complaint theme

Churn-risk index = complaint volume share × share of that theme's complaints containing explicit cancel/switch language, re-normalized to 100. Bar length = churn risk; label shows illustrative $ revenue at risk.
Volume shares and churn-language shares are analyst estimates calibrated to the sourced qualitative evidence (see Evidence panel) — not a raw scraped review count. Revenue-at-risk assumes 3% of the 2.5M base churns annually for complaint-attributable reasons (tunable; WHOOP itself reports "record low churn" overall, so treat this as a sensitivity scenario, not an observed figure).

Volume vs. churn-intent quadrant

X = share of total complaint volume. Y = % of that theme's complaints containing cancel/switch language. Bubble size = severity (1–5). Top-right = both loud and churn-driving.
This is the single most decision-relevant chart: themes in the upper-left (high volume, low churn intent — e.g. recovery accuracy) deserve UX/communication fixes; themes in the lower-right or far-right (lower volume, high churn intent — subscription, hardware, BPI/FDA) deserve retention and legal/compliance prioritization.

Complaint intensity over time, keyed to known events

Illustrative relative index (0–100), calibrated month-by-month to the sourced pattern of press coverage, FDA filings, and forum/review activity described in the underlying research — not raw scraped counts (see caveat below).
Data note: a production version of this chart would replace the modeled index with actual daily/weekly review-volume counts pulled from the App Store RSS feed, Google Play, Trustpilot, and Reddit's public API once that pipeline exists. The shape here is calibrated to reflect what the sourced research documents (e.g. "spiked hardest May 2025," "remains live in 2026 Trustpilot reviews," FDA warning July 2025 → closeout June 2026) rather than independently measured.

Evidence: sourced, verbatim complaint samples

A best-effort real-data sample — curated, attributed quotes from press coverage, FDA/court filings, WHOOP's own community forum and support docs, and review platforms. Bulk scraping of the App Store/Google Play/Trustpilot/Reddit at scale was attempted but not reliably accessible from this environment (rate limits / bot protections); this is a transparent, sourced sample rather than a full corpus.
